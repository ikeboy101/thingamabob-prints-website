THINGAMABOB PRINTS — WEBSITE PACKAGE

Pages
- index.html: Home / business introduction and featured product photos
- gallery.html: portfolio of finished prints; not every item is for sale
- shop.html: current purchases routed to the Etsy shop
- contact.html: general inquiries and custom print requests in one form

Brand direction
- Clean, premium layout with sage accents and warm neutral backgrounds
- Existing star mark retained as a simple text/star brand mark pending final logo artwork
- Real product photographs supplied by the owner are in assets/products/

Important setup
- Etsy shop: https://thingamabobprints.etsy.com
- Contact form currently prepares a message in the visitor's email app. The contact form is configured to prepare messages addressed to thingamabobprints@gmail.com. Visitors submit by opening their device's email app and pressing Send; this static site does not store form submissions.
- The site is static; form submissions are not stored on a server. A hosted form service can be added later if desired.

Publish
Upload the contents of this ZIP to the existing Netlify site. Keep index.html at the ZIP root.
